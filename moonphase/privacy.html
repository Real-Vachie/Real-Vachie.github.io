Moonphase privacy policy

Last updated: 19 October 2026

Moonphase is a live wallpaper that shows tonight's Moon. It has no accounts, no ads and no analytics, and it asks for no permissions except internet access.

What stays on your phone

Your settings, the city you choose, and the last weather reading are saved only on your phone. Android may include them in your own phone backup to your Google account if you have backup turned on. We (the developer) never receive them.

What leaves your phone, and why

  • City search. When you type a city, the name you type is sent to your phone's built-in location lookup (Android's Geocoder, usually run by Google) to find it on the map. See Google's privacy policy.
  • Weather. If you pick a city, its approximate coordinates (rounded to about 10 km) are sent to our small forecast relay (run on Cloudflare), which passes them on to the Norwegian Meteorological Institute (MET Norway, api.met.no) and keeps the forecast for that area for a while so it can answer other phones nearby. It asks at most about every 30 minutes, and only while the wallpaper is on screen. Nothing else about you is sent, and we don't keep a record of who asked; like any web server, the relay sees your IP address. See Cloudflare's privacy policy and MET Norway's privacy statement.
  • Moonphase+ purchase. Buying Moonphase+ is handled entirely by Google Play. We never see your payment details. See Google's privacy policy.

The only data that leaves your phone for us is the rounded city spot above, used just to fetch the weather and not kept, sold or shared for anything else. We don't use your device location (GPS); the Moon and weather follow the city you type.

Children

Moonphase is not directed at children and collects no personal information from anyone.

Deleting your data

Clear the city in the app, or uninstall Moonphase, and everything it saved is gone.

Changes and contact

If this policy changes, the new version will be posted here with a new date. Questions: use the contact email on the Moonphase Google Play page.
